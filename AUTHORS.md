## A note on the published version

This project began as an internal governed data API. The public version
differs in two ways:

1. **Identifiers replaced.** Database names, logins, roles, hostnames, and
   connection-string environment keys are placeholders throughout.
2. **Generated content removed.** The full entity map of the internal
   databases and the generated stored-procedure description catalogue are not
   included — they documented a private schema rather than the tool.

The API, the CI gates, and the deployment manifests are unchanged.
